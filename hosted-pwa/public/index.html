One tap, straight to cursor

Tap to start recording, tap to stop. VoiceBridge transcribes in the cloud and delivers text directly to the active input field on your computer — no window switching.

Multi-device

One phone, many computers

Sign in on multiple computers with the same account. Your phone auto-discovers all online devices — pick a target and send.

Fallback

Clipboard backup

When auto-paste isn't available, text is still copied to your computer's clipboard. Nothing is lost.

Long text, one phrase

Save repetitive content as commands. Say a phrase and get the whole thing typed out.
